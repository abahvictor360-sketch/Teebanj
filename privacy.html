Privacy

Home / Privacy

Teebanj Fashion World stores the contact and delivery information you submit to process orders and respond to enquiries. Account passwords are stored as secure hashes.

Card details are entered on Square's hosted checkout and are not stored by this website. Square processes payment information under its own privacy terms.

This website uses a session cookie for sign-in and checkout security, and browser storage to remember your bag and wishlist. Newsletter sign-up records your email and consent.

To ask about your information, request deletion or unsubscribe, email info@teebanjfashionworld.ca. Some transaction information may need to be retained for business records.
