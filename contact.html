Good to know

Delivery

Available destinations, delivery charges and taxes are shown during checkout. For international or custom orders, contact us before ordering.

Custom pieces

Tell us about your design, fabric, measurements and event date. We will discuss availability and a quote with you.

Returns

Please contact us before returning an item. Sale, worn, washed, damaged and personalised items may not be eligible. If an item arrives damaged or incorrect, contact us within seven days.

Payment and stock

Payments are processed securely by Square. Adding an item to your bag does not reserve it. We confirm your order after successful payment.
